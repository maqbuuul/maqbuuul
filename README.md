# Abdiwahid Ali
## Data Engineer — Customer & Marketing Data

**I connect marketing, customers, and revenue.**

I'm a Customer & Marketing Data Engineer working across marketing, customer, and revenue data. I started in advertising, where I learned that getting a click is easy; proving what it produced is not.

Today, I build the systems that close that gap — from tracking and data pipelines to trusted data models, attribution, customer analytics, and revenue analysis.

My work sits at the intersection of data engineering, analytics engineering, marketing measurement, and customer intelligence.

I care about building systems where a number can be traced back to what actually happened in the business.

Ad click → Lead → Customer → Booking → Attendance → Revenue

## What I build

* **Marketing data pipelines** — collecting and transforming data from advertising platforms, analytics tools, CRMs, and operational systems.
* **Customer data systems** — unified customer views, segmentation, cohorts, retention, churn, LTV, and next-purchase analysis.
* **Marketing attribution** — connecting acquisition activity to downstream customer outcomes rather than stopping at leads or platform conversions.
* **Revenue reconciliation** — connecting transactions, payments, bookings, and operational records to produce more defensible revenue numbers.
* **Analytics engineering** — warehouses, dbt models, data quality tests, transformations, and business-ready datasets.
* **ML systems** — customer segmentation, churn, LTV, demand forecasting, stockout risk, and predictive scoring.
* **Reverse ETL & activation** — sending warehouse-derived customer segments and predictions back into CRMs and marketing platforms.
* **Data products** — turning pipelines and analysis into tools that people can actually use.
